Questions,

How accurate is a photo-based nutrition estimate?+

A photo can’t show exact ingredients, cooking oil, or weight. NutriMealSnap offers an estimate that you can review and adjust; it shouldn’t be treated as a lab measurement.

Can I edit a meal before saving it?+

Yes. Review the detected foods and portion estimates, make changes, and confirm before adding the meal to your log.

Does NutriMealSnap provide medical advice?+

No. The app is intended for general nutrition tracking and is not a substitute for advice from a qualified healthcare professional.

How does the Family plan work?+

The planned Family setup is for an owner plus up to three invited members, each using a separate account. Family billing and invitations are not available yet.

When can I subscribe?+

Subscription purchase options are not live yet. When enabled, the app will show the price and currency returned by your Google Play storefront.

Privacy Policy

NutriMealSnap uses account information to provide your profile and sync your food and water logs. If you submit a meal for analysis, the photo is sent to the analysis service; a compressed photo is stored privately only after you confirm the scan. Nutrition estimates and logs are associated with your account. You control what you submit and can contact the operator with privacy questions.

Some data may be processed by service providers used for sign-in, meal analysis, nutrition references, and push notifications. Exact handling depends on the app version and services configured at launch. We’ll publish the complete operational privacy policy and contact details before public release.

Terms of Use

NutriMealSnap provides food recognition and nutrition estimates for general tracking. Results can be incomplete or inaccurate because a photo may not show ingredients, recipe variations, or exact portions. Review and edit results before relying on them.

The app is not a medical device and does not provide diagnosis or treatment. Ask a qualified professional for advice about health conditions, dietary restrictions, or nutrition decisions. Subscription availability, features, and pricing are subject to the terms shown by the relevant app store at purchase.
